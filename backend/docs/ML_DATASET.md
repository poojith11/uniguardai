# UNIGUARD AI (SIH26145) — ML Dataset & Preprocessing Specification (Phase 3A)

## 1. Selected Datasets & Authoritative Verification

### Primary Dataset: NF-CSE-CIC-IDS2018-v2
* **Source**: University of Queensland (UQ-NIDS) & Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity (CIC).
* **Format**: Standardized NetFlow / IPFIX flow records (.parquet / .csv) and original raw PCAP packet captures.
* **Size**: 18,893,708 total flows across 10 capture days.
* **Licensing**: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) for academic and research use.
* **Citation**: Sarhan, M., Layeghy, S., & Portmann, M. (2021). *Towards a Standard Feature Set for Network Anomaly Detection*. IEEE Access.
* **Why Selected as Primary**:
  * Generated from forward/backward flow aggregation matching NetFlow standards.
  * Contains line-rate enterprise traffic and 14 distinct cyberattack scenarios.
  * Captures actual transport layer metrics without synthetic generation.

---

### Supplementary / Evaluation Dataset: CTU-13 (Botnet Traffic)
* **Source**: Czech Technical University (CTU) Stratosphere Research Group.
* **Format**: Raw `.pcap` files and labeled `.binetflow` flow files generated by Argus (BiArgus).
* **Size**: 13 captured real botnet scenarios across infected physical hosts.
* **Licensing**: Open research license (Stratosphere Laboratory).
* **Directional Mismatch & Scientific Limitation**:
  > [!WARNING]
  > **Directionality Mismatch Notice**: The official pre-extracted CTU-13 `.binetflow` files use bidirectional flow aggregation (BiArgus). The CTU authors explicitly document the differences and limitations of older unidirectional flow models versus bidirectional graphs. Therefore, **CTU-13 pre-extracted flow files cannot be directly mixed with our unidirectional feature vectors without re-extracting the raw PCAP files using our Phase 2 unidirectional parser**. In UNIGUARD AI, CTU-13 is treated strictly as a specialized raw PCAP evaluation and supplementary data source.

## 3. Class Distribution Analysis

### A. Original Full Dataset Distribution (NF-CSE-CIC-IDS2018-v2, ~18.89M Flows)
* `Benign`: **~16,635,567 flows (88.0%)**
* `DDoS / DoS Attacks`: **~2,173,441 flows (11.5%)**
* `Brute-Force (FTP/SSH)`: **~380,943 flows (2.0%)**
* `Infiltration / Recon`: **~161,934 flows (0.85%)**
* `Bot (C2 Ares)`: **~286,191 flows (1.51%)**
* `Exfiltration`: **0 flows (0.0%)**

### B. Selected Working Subset (Candidate Sampling for Phase 3B)
For model development and training, a stratified working sample of **250,000 to 500,000 flows** will be selected to maintain fast training turnaround and memory efficiency.

### C. Proposed Final Training Composition (Target Proportion)
To prevent majority-class overdominance during model optimization, the proposed balanced training corpus target is:
* `BENIGN`: **~70%**
* `DDOS`: **~10%**
* `RECON_SCAN`: **~10%**
* `C2_BEACONING`: **~5%**
* `EXFILTRATION`: **~5%** (Ingested from supplementary raw PCAPs)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The target 70/10/10/5/5 distribution is a proposed training composition, **not the raw dataset distribution**. The final training dataset has not been created or sampled yet.

## 4. Authoritative Feature Breakdown (35 Total Fields)

Our Phase 2 feature extractor generates **35 total fields**:
* **8 Identifiers & Metadata Fields** (Excluded from model training to prevent memorization leakage).
* **27 Canonical Predictive ML Features** (Derived deterministically).

### Feature Breakdown:
```
[ 35 Total Extracted Fields ]
├── Identifiers & Metadata (8) [EXCLUDED FROM ML TRAINING]
│   ├── flow_id, src_ip, dst_ip, src_port, dst_port, protocol, start_time, end_time
│
└── Canonical Predictive Features (27) [INPUTS TO ML MODEL]
    ├── Volume & Timing Dynamics (5)
    │   ├── duration_seconds, packet_count, byte_count, packets_per_second, bytes_per_second
    │
    ├── Packet Size Distribution & Entropy (5)
    │   ├── mean_packet_size, std_packet_size, min_packet_size, max_packet_size, packet_size_entropy
    │
    ├── Inter-Arrival Time Dynamics (6)
    │   ├── mean_iat_seconds, std_iat_seconds, min_iat_seconds, max_iat_seconds, iat_jitter_seconds, burstiness_ratio
    │
    ├── TCP Control Flag Ratios & Counts (9)
    │   ├── syn_count, ack_count, fin_count, rst_count, psh_count, urg_count, syn_ratio, ack_ratio, rst_ratio
    │
    └── Contextual Binary Port Flags (2)
        ├── is_well_known_dst_port, is_ephemeral_src_port
```

## 5. Data Leakage Defenses

1. **Identifier Stripping**: `FORBIDDEN_LEAKAGE_COLUMNS` automatically removes IP addresses, MACs, timestamps, and flow IDs.
2. **Scaler Isolation**: `StandardScaler` / `RobustScaler` is fitted **strictly on `X_train`** and applied to validation and test sets.
3. **Split Isolation**: Train/Test split verification verifies zero entity overlap across data subsets.
